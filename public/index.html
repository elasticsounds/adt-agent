Connect your OpenAI key

Your OpenAI API key is required for PDF analysis and design generation. It also powers AI-assisted book edits and image descriptions. Files are processed locally; only the context you approve is sent directly to OpenAI. Full-PDF analysis and page-editing chat each have a separate sharing control.

Create or manage your OpenAI keys ↗

Remembered keys are encrypted in this browser, separately from your projects. They survive refresh and reopening this site. Keys are never included in exports or sent to our server. Disconnect removes the saved key. Use a personal project key with a spending limit.
